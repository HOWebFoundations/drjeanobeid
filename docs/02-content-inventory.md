## Production process

Copy is drafted by HOWF from **two structured 60-minute interviews** with the doctor. He
reviews and approves every medical page.

**No medical copy goes live without his sign-off** — for liability, and because search
credibility on YMYL content depends on a real, credentialed, verifiable author.

[Likely] **the doctor's review time is the critical path and where this project slips.**
Every gate gets an agreed five-business-day review window, agreed up front, in writing,
before Week 1 closes.

## Editorial rules

- **Answer-first openings.** The first paragraph answers the question in the page title.
  Everything else is supporting detail. This serves the anxious reader and the AI answer
  engines equally.
- **No superlatives, no comparisons, no testimonials, no pricing promotions** until the
  Lebanese Order of Physicians advertising rules are confirmed (see Diligence). [Guessing]
  these are restricted. Write conservatively now rather than rewriting later.
- **Entity facts must be identical** across the site, Google Business Profile, and every
  directory profile: name spelling, address, phone, hours, credentials. Inconsistency here
  quietly costs local rankings.
- **No medical text trapped inside the 3D canvas.** Anything a search engine or screen
  reader needs must be in server-rendered HTML.
- **Written consent required** for any photograph showing a patient.
