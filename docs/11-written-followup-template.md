## HOWF notes — delete before sending

- **Format to send:** a Word document or Google Doc he can type into, not this Markdown. One
  condition per page, so it doesn't look like one long form.
- **The italic prompts** under question 2 for cholesterol, palpitations and syncope are there
  because those three don't fit "which symptoms" cleanly. Without a nudge, a careful doctor
  stalls on the question.
- **Question 6 becomes the FAQ** on each page, and feeds the `FAQPage` schema. Push for real
  patient wording over clinical phrasing.
- **When it comes back:** draft the five pages, then send them for medical review in the same
  batch as the other eleven, inside the five-day window. Don't send them one at a time.
- **If it hasn't come back in five business days:** offer a 30-minute call to do it by voice
  instead. Some doctors find talking far quicker than writing, and a stalled template is the
  most common way this phase slips.
