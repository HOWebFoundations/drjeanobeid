# 05 — Delivery plan

Nine weeks to launch. Every gate carries an agreed **five-business-day doctor review
window**, agreed in writing before Week 1 closes.

## Week 1 — Discovery

- Doctor interview (first of two structured 60-minute sessions)
- Access: domain, DNS, hosting, Google Business Profile, analytics
- Directory audit (Vezeeta, DRAPP, any others)
- Keyword map in both launch languages
- Redirect inventory from the current drobeid.com

**Gate: scope and language pair signed.** Nothing in Week 2 starts until this closes and
the open questions in `06-diligence.md` are answered.

## Weeks 2–3 — Design and copy

- Information architecture
- Copy drafts (second doctor interview)
- Photoshoot booked
- Design system + three key screens: home, condition, contact

**In parallel:** heart reference stills, 3D generation, first doctor review of the mesh.

## Weeks 4–6 — Build

- All templates
- i18n
- Forms
- Schema
- Analytics
- Heart video integrated
- **Desktop 3D viewer built last** — it is the most cuttable item, so it is the last thing
  we spend time on

## Week 7 — Review and QA

- Content load
- **Doctor review of every medical page** (the critical path — see below)
- Accessibility pass
- Performance pass against the budgets in `04-technical-architecture.md`
- Cross-device QA on iPhone and the Android tablet

## Week 8 — Launch

- Redirects live
- Sitemap submitted
- GBP and directory profiles updated to point at the site
- Monitoring on

## Week 9 onward

- First article batch
- 30-day data review

---

## The critical path

[Likely] **the doctor's review time is where this project slips.** Not the build, not the
heart, not the copy drafting — the waiting.

Mitigations:
- Five-business-day review windows agreed up front, per gate
- Review requests batched, never drip-fed
- Annotated frames and marked-up drafts, so reviewing is marking rather than composing
- The Week 7 medical review is the big one: get pages to him progressively from Week 5
  rather than all at once in Week 7

## After launch

| Item | Cadence |
| --- | --- |
| Events on every call, WhatsApp, form action | Live from launch |
| One-page report | Monthly |
| Articles | Two per month, under retainer |
| Technical audit | Quarterly |

**The retainer is where the recurring revenue in this engagement lives, not in the build.**
The build is a fixed-fee event; the retainer is the business. Structure the Week 1
conversation accordingly.
